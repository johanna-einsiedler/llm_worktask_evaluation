### **Common Pitfalls and Tricky Elements**

1. **Performance Bug Disguised as Complex Logic:**
   - The 1000-iteration loop appears to be intentional calculation logic
   - Candidates may try to optimize other parts instead of removing the loop
   - The division by 1000 at the end is a clue that the loop is unnecessary

2. **Low Stock Threshold Confusion:**
   - Requirements specify two different thresholds: <15 for reports, <10 for alerts
   - Candidates must implement the correct threshold (10) for the new feature

3. **File Format Precision:**
   - The alert file format must be exact: "PRODUCT_ID,PRODUCT_NAME,CURRENT_QTY,REORDER_QTY"
   - No spaces, specific order, CSV format

4. **Menu Integration:**
   - New feature must be menu option "5", not appended to existing options
   - Must maintain existing menu structure and flow

### **Evaluation Scoring Guide**

**80%+ Performance Indicators:**

**Below 80% Indicators:**
