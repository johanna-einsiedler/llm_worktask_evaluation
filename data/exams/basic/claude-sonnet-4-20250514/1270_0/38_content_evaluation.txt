## Detailed Answer Derivation

### Step-by-Step Transaction Processing

Processing transactions in chronological order from the provided CSV:

**Initial Inventory:**
- A001: 100, A002: 75, B001: 200, B002: 40, C001: 150

**Transaction Processing:**

1. **T001** (09:00): A001 SHIP +50 → A001: 100 + 50 = 150 ✅
2. **T002** (09:15): B001 SALE -25 → B001: 200 - 25 = 175 ✅
3. **T003** (09:30): A002 SHIP +30 → A002: 75 + 30 = 105 ✅
4. **T004** (09:45): C001 SALE -60 → C001: 150 - 60 = 90 ✅
5. **T005** (10:00): A001 SALE -80 → A001: 150 - 80 = 70 ✅
6. **T006** (10:15): B002 SALE -30 → B002: 40 - 30 = 10 ✅
7. **T007** (10:30): B002 SALE -30 → B002: 10 - 30 = -20 ❌ **REJECTED** (Insufficient inventory)
8. **T008** (10:45): B001 SHIP +75 → B001: 175 + 75 = 250 ✅
9. **T009** (11:00): C001 SALE -45 → C001: 90 - 45 = 45 ✅
10. **T010** (11:15): B002 SALE -5 → B002: 10 - 5 = 5 ✅

### Final Inventory Calculations

**A001 (Widget Alpha)**: 100 + 50 - 80 = **70**
**A002 (Widget Beta)**: 75 + 30 = **105** 
**B001 (Gadget Pro)**: 200 - 25 + 75 = **250**
**B002 (Gadget Lite)**: 40 - 30 - 5 = **5** (T007 rejected)
**C001 (Tool Master)**: 150 - 60 - 45 = **45**

### Low Stock Alert Analysis

Items where current_quantity < reorder_threshold:
- **A001**: 70 ≥ 25 (No alert)
- **A002**: 105 ≥ 30 (No alert)
- **B001**: 250 ≥ 50 (No alert)
- **B002**: 5 < 25 ✅ (Alert required)
- **C001**: 45 < 40 ✅ (Alert required)

### Rejected Transactions Analysis

**T007**: B002 SALE 30
- Current B002 stock at time of T007: 10 (after T006)
- Attempted sale: 30
- Result would be: 10 - 30 = -20 (negative inventory not allowed)
- **Status**: REJECTED - "Insufficient inventory"

### Daily Summary Calculations

- **Total transactions processed**: 9 (10 attempted - 1 rejected)
- **Total shipments**: 3 (T001, T003, T008)
- **Total sales**: 6 (T002, T004, T005, T006, T009, T010)

## Passing Criteria and Scoring Rubric

### Overall Passing Threshold: 80%

**Total Points: 100**
**Minimum Passing Score: 80 points**

### Detailed Scoring Breakdown

**1. Final Inventory Calculations (40 points)**
- All 5 items calculated correctly: 40 points
- 4 items correct: 32 points
- 3 items correct: 24 points
- 2 items correct: 16 points
- 1 item correct: 8 points
- 0 items correct: 0 points

**2. Transaction Validation and Rejection Logic (25 points)**
- T007 correctly identified as rejected with proper reason: 25 points

- T007 not identified as rejected: 0 points

**3. Low Stock Alert Generation (15 points)**

**4. Daily Summary Accuracy (15 points)**
- All 4 summary counts correct: 15 points
- 3 out of 4 counts correct: 11 points
- 2 out of 4 counts correct: 7 points
- 1 out of 4 counts correct: 3 points
- No counts correct: 0 points

**5. Code Quality and Execution (5 points)**
- Code executes without errors and produces correct output: 5 points
- Code executes with minor issues but produces mostly correct results: 3 points
- Code partially functional: 1 point
- Code doesn't execute or produces no meaningful output: 0 points

### Critical Success Requirements

To achieve a passing score (80+), candidates must demonstrate:

1. **Core Programming Competency**: Accurate processing of at least 4 out of 5 inventory calculations (32+ points)
2. **Business Logic Implementation**: Proper identification of T007 rejection (25 points)
3. **Data Processing Skills**: Accurate daily summary calculations (11+ points)
4. **Basic Alert Logic**: At least one correct low stock identification (10+ points)
5. **Functional Code**: Working program that executes (3+ points)

**Minimum combination for passing**: 32 + 25 + 11 + 10 + 3 = 81 points ✅

### Key Assessment Points

**Must-Have for Passing (80+ points):**
- Perfect or near-perfect inventory calculations (32-40 points)
- Correct transaction rejection logic (25 points)

### Automated Checking Points

For automated evaluation, the system should verify:

1. **JSON Structure**: Proper formatting and required fields present
2. **Data Types**: Integers for quantities, strings for codes/names
3. **Array Ordering**: Alphabetical by item_code where specified
4. **Calculation Accuracy**: Mathematical correctness of inventory updates
5. **Business Rule Compliance**: Proper rejection of negative inventory transactions
6. **Alert Logic**: Correct identification of items below reorder threshold

This evaluation framework ensures that candidates who pass demonstrate the essential programming skills needed for entry-level computer programming positions, including data processing, business logic implementation, error handling, and accurate mathematical calculations.
