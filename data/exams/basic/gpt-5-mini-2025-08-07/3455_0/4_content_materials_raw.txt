Potential traps and common candidate errors
- Treating the malformed timestamp as valid rather than skipping it will change rows_loaded and aggregates. The timestamp 2024-03-01T25:00:00Z is intentionally invalid (hour 25).
- Not deduplicating the exact duplicate row will cause host-03 averages to change; the duplicate is identical to an earlier row and must be skipped per expected grader rule.
- Averaging with NULL values: candidates might include NULL as zero if they coerce missing cpu_percent to 0 — the grader expects NULLs to be excluded from averages.
- Hour bucketing off-by-one: ensure hour_start is the top-of-hour truncation in UTC (e.g., 06:10 belongs to 06:00 hour_start).
- Spike detection requires per-host ordering by timestamp and detecting contiguous sequences of samples >90; counting samples across hosts or unordered data will produce false results.
